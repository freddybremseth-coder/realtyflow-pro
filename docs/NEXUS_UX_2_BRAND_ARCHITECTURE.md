# Nexus OS UX 2.0 — Brand & Channel Architecture

## Product principle

RealtyFlow should feel simple even when Nexus OS is complex underneath. The user-facing operating model is action-first: Today → Contacts → Properties → Marketing → Nexus, while advanced runtime, approvals, agents and diagnostics remain available behind Nexus OS.

## Canonical brand roles

### Freddy Bremseth
Professional expertise umbrella. This brand may selectively amplify the strongest stories from owned brands, but should not mechanically duplicate identical posts. Main themes include expertise and analysis, books, Spain/property advisory, AI/business building, entrepreneurship and selected brand stories.

The private Facebook profile is not an automated commercial publishing destination.

### Freddy Publishing
Dedicated publishing brand for books, series, sample chapters, catalog discovery and publishing news. Cross-posting to the Freddy Bremseth umbrella must be rewritten with a personal/expert angle rather than copied verbatim.

### Freddy AI Products
Dedicated product/technology brand for RealtyFlow, Nexus OS and future AI products. Capability, pricing, integrations and outcome claims require verified source data before publication. The current website entry is temporary until a dedicated verified product destination is registered.

## Nexus Today

Nexus Today is a presentation and decision layer, not a competing intelligence engine. It reuses:

- `/api/os/status` for canonical operational attention
- `/api/revenue/today` for sales priorities and next-best-action
- `/api/nexus/portfolio` for brand/channel portfolio state

This keeps one source of truth for priority scoring while making the system easier to operate.

## Autonomy rule

New Freddy Publishing and Freddy AI Meta channels remain planned but not pilot-enabled until the actual accounts are connected and write governance has been verified. Existing controlled pilot channels are unchanged by this UX phase.
